
# Adobe AI Innovation Challenge | Red Rocks NIL Platform

**Product Management | AI Innovation | UX/UI Design | NIL Strategy**

## Project Overview

Designed an interactive NIL partnership platform prototype for the University of Utah Red Rocks Gymnastics program as part of the Adobe Red Rocks AI Innovation Challenge. The proposed solution focuses on expanding sponsorship opportunities, increasing NIL revenue potential, and simplifying collaboration between student-athletes and brands.

The platform combines AI-assisted athlete storytelling, sponsor discovery, partnership management, and performance analytics to help athletes showcase their personal brands while enabling sponsors to identify partnership opportunities and evaluate their impact.

## Key Features

- **Athlete Story Studio:** AI-assisted tools to help athletes develop their personal brands and sponsorship content.
- **Sponsor Discovery:** Helps identify potential brand partnerships and sponsorship opportunities.
- **NIL Partnership Hub:** Centralizes partnership opportunities and sponsorship workflows.
- **Sponsor Impact Dashboard:** Provides visibility into partnership performance and engagement.

## Product Development & Design

Developed a clickable Figma prototype demonstrating athlete, sponsor, and administrative experiences. The concept emphasizes user-centered design, sponsorship revenue opportunities, and a scalable approach to NIL partnership management.
